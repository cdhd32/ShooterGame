RATION RUN
==========

Survive for 90 seconds and destroy incoming drones.

Controls
WASD       Move
Mouse      Look
Shift      Sprint
Left Click Shoot
R          Restart after win/game over
Esc        Quit

The on-screen HUD shows HP, score, remaining time, and a centered + crosshair.
Win/game-over results are shown in the game window; press R to restart.
Requires 64-bit Windows 10/11 and a DirectX 12 capable GPU.
